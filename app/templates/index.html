Zero-Device Biometric Payments

Authorize payments with your face or fingerprint — no card, no phone, no dedicated terminal. Biometrics are captured from your camera or an uploaded image and compared for real (OpenCV); only the bank is mocked, so it runs entirely on your machine.

Create an account Customer login Merchant login

1 · Enrol

Capture a face/fingerprint from camera or upload. Templates are encrypted with Fernet.

2 · Authenticate

Pay with face or fingerprint; the captured image is really compared to your template.

3 · Collect

A merchant charges you by account ID; the mock bank atomically moves the funds.
